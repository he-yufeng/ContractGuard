RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into between Marina View Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Dana Reyes ("Tenant") for the premises at 814 Bay Street, Unit 3, Alameda, California.

1. TERM. The lease term is twelve months, beginning May 1, 2026 and ending April 30, 2027.

2. RENT. Monthly rent shall be $3,200.00, due on the first day of each month. A late charge of 5% applies after a three-day grace period.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $6,400.00 before taking possession.

4. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord shall have the right to enter the Property at any time, with or without notice, for inspection, repairs, or showings.

5. RETURN OF DEPOSIT. Landlord shall return the security deposit within 21 days of move-out, with an itemized statement of any deductions.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity and internet service. Landlord provides water and trash service.

7. PETS. No pets are permitted without Landlord's prior written consent.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of California.
