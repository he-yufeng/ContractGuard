RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into between Golden Gate Homes LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Chen ("Tenant") for the premises at 290 Waller Street, San Francisco, California.

1. TERM. The lease term is twelve months, beginning June 1, 2026 and ending May 31, 2027.

2. RENT. Monthly rent shall be $3,200.00, due on the first day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,200.00 before move-in.

4. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Property with at least 24 hours' written notice to Tenant, during normal business hours, for inspection, repairs, or showings.

5. RETURN OF DEPOSIT. Landlord shall return the security deposit within 21 days of move-out, with an itemized statement of any deductions and remaining balance.

6. MAINTENANCE. Landlord shall maintain the premises in habitable condition as required by California law.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of California.
