RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into between Harbor Point Rentals Inc. ("Landlord") and Sam Ortiz ("Tenant") for the premises at 77 Channel Street, Oakland, California.

1. TERM. The lease term is twelve months, beginning July 1, 2026.

2. RENT. Monthly rent shall be $2,000.00, due on the first day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two months' rent. The security deposit is non-refundable and shall be retained by Landlord upon termination for any reason, including normal wear.

4. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Property upon 12 hours' notice to Tenant at any time of day.

5. RETURN OF DEPOSIT. Landlord shall refund the security deposit within 45 days after termination, less any amounts Landlord determines are owed.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant pays all utilities without exception.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of California.
